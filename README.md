# Superstore Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview
An end-to-end Power BI project analysing 9,994 rows of retail sales data.
Built three interactive dashboard pages covering performance overview,
profit problem analysis, and business recommendations.

## Dataset
Sample Superstore — public retail dataset with 9,994 rows covering
orders, sales, profit, regions, product categories and sub-categories.

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX measures
- Microsoft Excel (data preparation)

## Dashboard Pages

### Page 1 — Overview
- Total sales: $2.3M
- Total profit: $286K
- Sales and profit by region
- Sales and profit by category
- Sales and profit by sub-category

### Page 2 — Profit Analysis
- Profit by sub-category showing loss-making products
- Discount by sub-category
- Comparison revealing relationship between discounting and profit loss

### Page 3 — Findings and Recommendations
- Written business findings
- Actionable recommendations for senior leadership

## Key Findings
- Despite $2.3M in total sales, profit is only $286K — a thin margin problem
- West region leads in both sales and profit
- Central has second highest sales but lowest profit — least efficient region
- Tables and Bookcases generate negative profit despite consistent sales
- Three distinct pricing scenarios identified:
  - Copiers: low discount, highest profit
  - Phones: moderate discount, still profitable due to strong margins
  - Furniture: high discounts on thin margins causing losses
- Tables retained as loss leader strategy to protect brand reputation

## Business Recommendations
- Review supplier costs and shipping on Tables and Bookcases
- Introduce minimum discount thresholds to protect margins
- Learn from Copiers pricing strategy and apply across other categories
- Investigate Central region operational inefficiencies
